# PSL frontend assets

The brand images are from PSL and WIRON's official websites. Downloaded 2026-11-03.

- `frontend/assets/images/psl-logo.webp`: https://static.tildacdn.com/tild6563-3937-4431-b865-393361373236/_PSL_.png
- `frontend/assets/images/wiron.webp`: https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3433-6635-4166-b033-613965316237/_301_.png
- `frontend/assets/images/matrix-logo.png`: https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3330-3932-4737-b634-386433643537/fixtile_png___1__2.png
- `frontend/assets/images/silflex-logo.png`: https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3832-6264-4632-b339-316131616566/silflex_logo_png___1.png
- `frontend/assets/images/dolotex-logo.png`: https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3562-3234-4164-a633-376332396239/____.png

WIRON artwork and the PSL logo are resized and encoded as WebP for delivery. FIXTILE and SILFLEX logos have transparent outer margins trimmed. No product photos are drawn or generated.

Brand presentations are independent of the product catalog. Products come from `/api/products/all`. There is no fabricated fallback inventory. Legacy clothing demo categories are excluded. When the API is unavailable, links to the official brand sites remain usable.
